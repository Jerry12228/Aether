## Contracts

- 延用01-01的CJS/root/错误摘要/validateAnchor。导出 `validateFeatures({root,data,sources,scope,allowUnmapped}) -> {errors,counts,unmapped,conflicts}` 与 `renderFeatureParity(data,result) -> string`。CLI `--features`只要求sources/features/requirements/roadmap，不读取environment；`--scope qt-windows`允许真实tracer子集；`--allow-unmapped`仅Task2用于显示待补需求，摘要必须包含unmapped且注明未通过最终映射门禁。最终不带这两选项运行。
- features根：`schemaVersion=1,capturedAt,purpose,scope{platforms,complete},features[],surfaces[],cases[],conflicts[]`。稳定唯一ID与确定性排序；平台枚举为helios-windows10、helios-windows11、selene-windows、selene-macos、selene-ios-ipados、selene-android、selene-linux。
- feature：`id,capability,platform,originalBehavior,anchors[],settingIds[],conditions{os,architecture,hardware},ownerTier(flutter-ui|shared-native|host-native|platform-adapter|development-tool),requirementIds[],primaryPhase,mappingState(mapped|needs-requirement),caseIds[],aetherBehavior,implementationEvidence[],buildEvidence[],automationEvidence[],hardwareEvidence[],hardwareTodoId|null,blockerIds[]`。anchor沿用01-01固定repo/commit/path/blob/symbol/行范围/短原文契约。
- surface：`id,repo,platform,kind(setting|shortcut|cli|permission|input|media|network|management|packaging),path,anchors[],inventoryKeys[],entries[{key,featureIds,disposition(mapped|implementation-detail|deprecated|outside-target),reason,anchors}],reviewed`。每个发现key恰有一项覆盖或有证据排除；非设置入口也登记独立行为，不能仅统计key数量。
- case：`id,featureIds,platform,preconditions,steps,expectedResults,negativeCases,evidenceRequired,verificationPhase,status(planned|passed|blocked),evidence[]`。本阶段产品案例为planned，不虚构已运行；工具测试才可passed。conflict：`id,featureIds,description,sourceEvidence,affectedConstraint,options,status(open|decided),decisionEvidence`。
- --report docs/FEATURE-PARITY.md在验证后生成可读矩阵，保留现有范围说明并链接数据；失败不覆盖报告。每次CLI须输出非空JSON摘要；所选链缺输入、未知状态或非法引用退出非零。
